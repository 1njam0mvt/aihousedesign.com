How these numbers are produced

Every figure is computed from the {{ '{:,}'.format(d.total) }} generated designs in the library. Cost is the planner's rough estimate: built area multiplied by a per-square-foot rate for the style. It is not a market quotation and excludes land, approvals, taxes and furnishing. Read the methodology for the rate table and the limits.
