Cookie Policy

Last updated: {{ LEGAL_UPDATED }}

{{ SITE_NAME }} uses essential browser storage to work, and optional analytics cookies only if you allow them. You can change your choice at any time.

What is saved in your browser

To remember your choices, the site uses your browser's local storage. This is similar to a cookie but is never sent to our server.

Name Purpose Kept
hd-consent / hd_consent Remembers your cookie choice (local storage and a first-party cookie) 1 year
_ga, _ga_* (optional) Google Analytics, set only if you accept analytics Up to 2 years
hd-theme Remembers light or dark mode Until you clear it
hd-lang Remembers your chosen language Until you clear it

Both are strictly necessary for the features you choose, and they contain no personal information.

Third party content

The 3D viewer loads the three.js library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). This is a normal request for a file, and your browser shares your IP address with that service. We do not control it. See our Privacy Policy.

How to clear it

In your browser settings, clear "site data" or "local storage" for this site. The site will then go back to your device's default theme and English.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising, we will ask for your permission first and update this page.
