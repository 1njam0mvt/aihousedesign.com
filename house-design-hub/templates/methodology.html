How plans are generated

A procedural planner works in feet. It takes the buildable rectangle of the plot and packs rooms (living, kitchen, dining, bedrooms, bathrooms, verandah, pooja, stair and more) into horizontal bands that fill the rectangle exactly, using relative area weights for each room type. Layouts are therefore gap-free and valid as concepts. The same inputs always give the same plan.

The designer reads your request with a rule-based parser that understands plot sizes such as 30x40, bedroom counts, floors and style words. If a language model is configured it can add short design notes, but the layout itself comes from the planner.

What estimates leave out

  • Land, registration and taxes
  • Approvals, drawings and professional fees
  • Furniture, appliances and landscaping unless added in the 3D builder
  • Local price changes, labour rates and the quality of finishes you choose

Limits you should know

  • Plans are concepts. They are not structural designs and do not apply your local building rules, setbacks or fire and safety codes.
  • The tools do not convert photos into 3D models.
  • Always have a licensed architect and structural engineer review and approve drawings before construction.

Data, privacy and corrections

The house design statistics are computed from the library and update when the library changes. Read the privacy policy to see what we collect. If you find a mistake in a plan, a number or a page, tell us through the contact page and we will review it and correct it.
